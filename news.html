SYSTEM LOG: MAY 24, 2024

POST HUMAN: NeX GEn is finally here

After multiple delays and intense studio sessions, Bring Me The Horizon has officially released the second phase of their ambitious POST HUMAN project. "NeX GEn" explores a complex narrative deeply rooted in post-hardcore and hyperpop aesthetics. Oliver Sykes stated that the record took longer than expected due to a desire for perfection and internal shifts within the band. The album is now streaming across all global platforms, with physical vinyl and cassette editions shipping next month.

SYSTEM LOG: APRIL 15, 2024

Massive World Tour Announced for 2024/2025

Prepare your physical forms. BMTH has announced their biggest global stadium and arena tour to date in support of the NeX GEn release. The tour will kick off in Australia before moving through major European cities, culminating in a massive North American leg. Support acts include Sleep Token, Bad Omens, and Cassyette. Pre-sale tickets will be available to mailing list subscribers starting this Friday. Do not miss the chance to experience the new visual production live.

SYSTEM LOG: JANUARY 9, 2024

New Heavy Single "Kool-Aid" Released

Dropping completely out of nowhere, "Kool-Aid" is the first taste of new music since the departure of longtime keyboardist Jordan Fish. The track has been praised by fans and critics alike for returning to a much heavier, aggressive guitar-centric sound reminiscent of the "Sempiternal" and "Survival Horror" eras. The accompanying music video, directed by Sykes, features intense cyberpunk imagery and a raw performance from the band.

SYSTEM LOG: DECEMBER 22, 2023

Official Statement: Jordan Fish Departure

In a brief official statement released across social media platforms, Bring Me The Horizon confirmed that they are parting ways with Jordan Fish. Fish joined the band in 2012 and was instrumental in shaping their electronic rock sound from "Sempiternal" onwards. The band stated they wish him the best in his future production endeavors and assured fans that the completion of the new album is still underway.
